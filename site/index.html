FAQ

Common questions.

What types of businesses do you work with?
Service-based businesses are a natural fit — home services, building maintenance, clinics and med spas, agencies, and other operations where leads, appointments, and follow-ups drive the day. If your team repeats the same manual steps every week, there's likely something worth automating.
What processes can you help automate?
Lead capture and CRM updates, appointment booking with calendar availability checks, customer follow-up messages, missed-call text back, review requests, team notifications, and data transfer between applications — the repetitive processes between the tools you already use.
What tools do you primarily use?
Make.com for workflow automation and integrations, GoHighLevel for CRM and lead management, and APIs for connecting calendars, databases, and other business apps. Portfolio projects have also used Supabase, Vercel, GitHub, and Google Sheets — the right tool depends on your existing setup.
Do I need to pay for the tools?
For the tools themselves, yes — you keep and pay for your own subscriptions, such as Make.com and GoHighLevel, along with any other tools your business already uses or needs. I build the automations and workflows on top of your accounts, so everything stays under your ownership and control. On the discovery call, we'll go over exactly which subscriptions your specific build needs before anything starts.
Do you build custom workflows?
Yes. Every workflow starts with your actual process: we map how work moves today, define what the automation should do, then build and test it — including what happens when something goes wrong, like a fully booked calendar.
How does the discovery call work?
It's a 30-minute conversation about your business, your current workflow, and what you'd like to improve. Book a time directly in the calendar on this page — it adjusts to your time zone — or message me on WhatsApp first if you prefer.
Can you work with international clients?
Yes. I'm based in the Philippines and available for international collaboration. My background includes supporting customers and operations for businesses abroad, and I'm used to coordinating across time zones.

Booking System & Make.com Automation

SwiftFix Building Maintenance Services

Biñan, Laguna, Philippines · Independent portfolio project

The Objective

Explore how a service-based business can simplify its booking process and coordinate appointments through a calendar-connected workflow — organizing customer inquiries, service requests, and scheduling in one place.

The Make.com Automation

  • Webhook receives each booking-form submission
  • Request logged and the requested schedule parsed
  • Calendar free/busy checked via API before any booking
  • Available path: event created, customer and admin emailed, Slack notified, status confirmed
  • Unavailable path: customer asked to rebook, status marked unavailable, team notified
  • One-hour scheduling windows, weekday rules, Philippine timezone (Asia/Manila)

Tools Used

Make.com · Supabase · API · Vercel · TanStack Start · JavaScript · Slack
